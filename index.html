Season 4 winning lineups (static snapshot)

Snapshot of 3,235 rated matches from the top-20 players on the Sep 24, 2026 2:41 PM PT leaderboard (matches Sep 19 12:07 AM – Sep 24 2:36 PM PT). By each match's season field, that is 2,611 Season 4, 417 Season 3, and 207 Season 2. The figures below were computed on all 3,235 matches, not Season 4 alone. Not live data.

  • No single deck dominates: 3,045 different boards across 5,684 lineups; the most-used bot is in only 5.9%.

In a pair such as 65.9% / 55.6%, the first figure is overall and the second excludes the heaviest attacker. Win rates are observational; they partly reflect who plays these picks, not only the picks.
