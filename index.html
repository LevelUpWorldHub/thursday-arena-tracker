Season 4 winning lineups (static snapshot)

Static snapshot of 2,611 rated Season 4 matches involving top-20 players, not every Season 4 match (about 9,165 rated Season 4 matches as of Sep 24, 6:45 PM PT). Window: Sep 23 12:05 AM – Sep 24 2:36 PM PT. 4,800 top-20 lineup-games from the Sep 24, 2026 2:41 PM PT leaderboard. Not live data.

  • No single deck dominates: 2,663 different boards across 4,800 lineup-games. The most-used bot, Vigil, is in 6.5% of those 4,800.
  • Common bots are in 99.8% of 4,800 lineup-games. Lineups with no active crew bonus won 15.7% (n=108).
  • Fusion lineups won 51.8% (n=4,258) versus 36.0% without Fusion (n=542). Without sodiumhyrdride that gap is 52.7% (n=1,773) versus 50.0% (n=142).
  • Charmer Fusion (Personal + Sales): 61.3% (n=481) overall and 62.2% (n=296) without sodiumhyrdride, consistent across both views.
  • Top 5 of 17 by the lower of the two win rates: X Hygiene 63.5% (n=159) / 66.3% (n=89); Meeting Recap Deck 61.0% (n=159) / 61.8% (n=76); Foundry 62.0% (n=184) / 60.3% (n=63); ideabot 71.2% (n=139) / 59.6% (n=52); Alchemist 64.2% (n=218) / 58.5% (n=106).
  • Credit Card Max is not a winner: 46.6% (n=249) overall and 44.0% (n=100) without sodiumhyrdride.
  • Add-ons: Double tap relic 65.9% (n=472) / 55.6% (n=223); Gearhead captain 61.2% (n=224) / 61.4% (n=140).
  • dr eggbot: 17 of 5,222 Season 4 sides (0.3%), record 4-11-2. It sat in the middle seat in 12 of 17 board-rounds.
  • One player (sodiumhyrdride) was the attacker in 52.7% of these matches (1,376 of 2,611), so these lineup findings are concentrated in that player's games. A pair such as 65.9% / 55.6% is overall, then the same cut with that player removed.

Win rates are observational; they partly reflect who plays these picks, not only the picks.
